# P0.2: TC15 and the unmodified verifier (proof class: Design, from recorded [M] runs)

Date: 2026-10-22. No new runs: this task restates what Phases 3 and 4 measured.

## Why this task exists

The ERC's "Where the tests live" said that TC13–TC15 passed with the example's unmodified verifier. Phase 4 measured
something narrower for TC15 (V6 stored results, V7 final retirement denials). Its Backwards Compatibility section and
evidence entry 19 said, more generally, that existing verifiers "inherit Core" and that the example's verifier
"conforms unchanged". That holds for Core's required verifier rules V1–V5. It does not hold for the two
recommendations V6 and V7.
