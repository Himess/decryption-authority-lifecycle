## Notes

1. **R6, B3a:** residual until revoked: a delegation A made before migrating stands (A is not retired). Closed by the holder's revocation through `execute` (U-R6).
2. **R6, B3b:** residual until revoked, as B3a. Closed by revocation (U-R6).
3. **E1-off, B3a:** residual: on this local chain the old key's grant (T1) is mined, and the attacker it names reads with its own valid key. Closed by a host chain enforcing EIP-7851 (the key's transactions invalid). Profile Q refuses this attacker (E1-on) only because it signs with ECDSA: a grant to an attacker's post-quantum wallet would be served (Phase 4 P1.2 §2.4, the asserted residual).
4. **E1-off, B3b:** residual: as B3a; closed only by a host chain enforcing EIP-8298 (anvil does not apply EIP-3607, which makes the key's transactions invalid once the account has regular code).
5. **E2-off, B3a:** residual: the old key's delegation to the attacker's KDA is mined and served. Closed by the host chain (EIP-7851), or by Profile Q after B_Q (E2-on).
6. **E2-off, B3b:** residual: as B3a. Profile Q does not close it (E2-on): only the host chain (EIP-8298).
7. **E2-on, B3b:** residual: the delegation was made after B_Q, but B3b's code was already regular at B_Q, so Q5 does not cut it off. On a host chain enforcing EIP-8298 the old key could not have sent it (EIP-3607); Q7 limits Profile Q's claims to such chains.
8. **P2, B2d:** refused by design: a successor's KDA does not reach A's handles (S9; D98).
9. **P2, B2c:** refused by design (S9; D98).
10. **P3, B1:** not followed by design: a grant to a retired account is inert (R8); the application grants to W, or A also names W as successor.
11. **T1, B3a:** residual: mined on this local chain (it accepts the old key's transactions). Closed by a host chain enforcing EIP-7851.
12. **T1, B3b:** residual: mined here (anvil does not apply EIP-3607 to the simulated adoption); closed by a host chain enforcing EIP-8298.
13. **T2, B3a:** residual: mined (publication is irreversible). Closed by EIP-7851 enforcement.
14. **T2, B3b:** residual: mined; closed by EIP-8298 enforcement.
15. **T3, B3a:** residual: mined; the holder can revoke it (U-E2), the old key can redo it. Closed by EIP-7851 enforcement.
16. **T3, B3b:** residual: mined; revocable by the holder; closed by EIP-8298 enforcement.
17. **T4, B3a:** residual: mined, but it must mature (D) and be accepted; the holder cancels it through `execute`. Closed by EIP-7851 enforcement.
18. **T4, B3b:** residual: as B3a; closed by EIP-8298 enforcement.
19. **T7, B3a:** residual (availability): the holder's permits signed before the time stop counting; the holder signs new ones.
20. **T7, B3b:** residual (availability), as B3a.
21. **T8, B3a:** residual (availability): the old key retires A, which ends the wallet's authority too (P4). Closed by EIP-7851 enforcement.
22. **T8, B3b:** residual (availability): as B3a; closed by EIP-8298 enforcement.
